Book

The Reserve

out now

Exercise physiology for longevity, written for coaches, physiotherapists and clinicians. Its starting point is that people do not lose function gradually: capacity declines smoothly, but independence goes in steps, on the day capacity falls below what an ordinary task demands. The book follows six reserves (aerobic capacity, strength, power, bone, balance and metabolism), shows where each one's threshold sits, and turns the evidence into training. Every effect size is sourced and graded: verified, probable or untraced.
