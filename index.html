About

Tomas Lebedevas is a physicist by training. At Vilnius University he earned a bachelor's degree in computational physics, followed by a master's in environmental and chemical physics. For more than a decade he has worked in medical technology, responsible for the information systems that hospitals and laboratories rely on every day. He is also a committed chess player: a blitz rating of 2030 on Chess.com places him among the top 2% of the platform's players worldwide.

The Reserve

Exercise physiology for longevity. The book looks at six physiological reserves (aerobic capacity, strength, power, bone, balance and metabolism), how each of them declines with age, and what training can do about it. Working manuscript, 15 chapters.
